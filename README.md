Used-Car-Analysis

OVERVIEW:
This project focuses on analyzing the used car market data across 5 major cities to understand pricing trends, brand distribution, and the factors influencing used car prices. The goal is to deliver actionable insights for businesses and consumers to make informed decisions.

Tech Stack:
- Python Libraries: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Tools: Jupyter Notebook, Power BI

Dataset:
- Datasets were collected from multiple sources covering 5 cities- Bangalore, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Hyderabad
- Key attributes: Car Age, Kilometers Driven, Brand, Fuel Type, Transmission, Ownership, and Price.

Objectives:
1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing: Handled missing values, standardized data formats, and created additional features for analysis.
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): 
   - Examined availability and distribution of cars across cities.
   - Visualized pricing trends by city, brand, and fuel type.
3. Correlation & Insights:
   - Analyzed impact of car age, driven kilometers, and ownership history on price.
   - Identified patterns to understand market dynamics.

Key Insights:
- Older vehicles and higher mileage significantly reduce resale price.
- Certain brands retained value better across geographies.
- First-owner cars showed 20-30% higher pricing compared to later owners.

Business Value:
- Helps dealers optimize pricing strategies and inventory.
- Provides insights for buyers to make better purchase decisions.
- Highlights factors affecting car value retention across markets.
